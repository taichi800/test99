Puckman Figure Ground Finder

On each round, Puckman is camouflaged inside a different environment. Your task is simply to find him and click as fast as you can. Difficulty changes how much he blends into the background. This is a practice and testing tool, not a clinical instrument.

Suggested protocol:
• Use a moderate difficulty first, then gradually increase blending if it feels comfortable.
• Treat each round as one trial: look at the scene, let your eyes explore, then click when you spot Puckman.
• If wrong clicks or search time climb sharply, reduce difficulty or stop. Excessive strain or frustration defeats the point of the exercise.
