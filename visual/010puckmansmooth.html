Visual Tracking - Puck Man Smooth Pursuit

This mode is like a small between-level scene: the puck wanders around in smooth, somewhat unpredictable paths. The idea is to follow it with your eyes while keeping your head still. This is an experiment, not a treatment plan. Stop if you get strong eye strain, headache, nausea, or worrying visual changes.
