Puckman Visual Closure – Fishing

Puckman is in a boat watching the water. Each trial, only the top of a fish sticks out of the water line. Your task is to spot this partial shape and click directly on it to pull up the fish. This is a practice tool for visual closure and figure–ground, not a medical test.

3 medium
Keep your head still, move only your eyes.
Click the partial fish when you spot it.
Clicks in the water without a fish count as errors.
Idle. Choose difficulty, set fish per session, then press Start fishing.

Suggested use:
• Use a mid level difficulty first. Watch the water band and let your eyes roam rather than staring at one point.
• When you see a hint of a fish body or fin above the ripple line, click that region to pull it up.
• The program shows one fish at a time. It tracks how many you catch, how many errors you make, and your average reaction time. If the session becomes tiring or uncomfortable, stop or lower the difficulty.
