Vergence Targets - Side by Side

This pattern gives two identical symbols with adjustable spacing. Some people use this kind of setup to practice making the two images overlap or fuse. This code is for experimentation only and is not a treatment plan. If you have diplopia or other eye conditions, a clinician should decide whether you should do any vergence work.

20% (~0 px) Higher value = targets farther apart horizontally.
40 px

Suggested use (not medical advice):
• Sit at a comfortable distance and keep your head still.
• Start with a moderate disparity. Look at the central marker between the two symbols.
• For crossed fusion (eyes turning more inward), some people slowly relax their gaze toward the marker until the two symbols seem to overlap into a third one in the middle.
• For through or parallel fusion (eyes aiming “through” the screen), some people imagine looking past the screen so the two symbols appear to drift together.
Stop if you get strong eye strain, headache, nausea, or any worrying change in your vision and discuss this kind of work with an eye care professional.
