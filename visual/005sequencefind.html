Crowded Search - Sequence Finder

This creates dense fields of letters and asks you to find specific sequences, such as all horizontal or vertical AB pairs. The idea is to stress visual discrimination and tracking under crowding. This is a practice tool, not a clinical test.

Target: A B · Orientation: horizontal right and vertical down
Sequences: 0 · Found: 0 · Remaining: 0 · Errors: 0 · Time: 0.0 s

Task suggestion:
- Pick a comfortable viewing distance and keep your head still, using only your eyes to scan.
- First pass: scan row by row and click any cell you believe starts the target sequence from left to right.
- Second pass: scan column by column and click any cell you believe starts the target sequence from top to bottom.
Each correct click highlights the whole sequence. Wrong clicks increment the error counter and briefly highlight the cell in red. Stop or change settings if the crowding or effort feels excessive.
